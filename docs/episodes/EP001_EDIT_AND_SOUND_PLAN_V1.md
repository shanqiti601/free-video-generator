## Audio and finishing

- Narration is the lead; music must never mask consonants or scientific names.
- Use original or explicitly reusable music and ambience only. No Planet Earth/BBC soundtrack, ripped audio, or unlicensed stock.
- Record/produce narration in one consistent voice; manually review pronunciation of *Albatrossia pectoralis*, chemosynthesis, hydrothermal, and bioluminescence.
- Use natural pauses and shot holds. No generic cinematic risers on every cut.
- Grade for natural colour; avoid crushing blacks or boosting blue saturation until species detail disappears.
- No full-screen paragraphs. On-screen text limited to chapter labels, factual depth markers, and the FACT BITES end card.
- Preserve source aspect ratio and frame rate; no claims of native 4K for an upscaled source.

## Final acceptance gates

- Full source clips watched and usable in/out points logged.
- Every asset has exact page, creator, credit, and reuse basis recorded.
- Scientific claims fact-checked against the script source ledger.
- Narration audio reviewed end-to-end for timing, pronunciation, clipping, and noise.
- Full MP4 decoded and watched; no black frames, broken transitions, audio sync issues, or title cards left unintentionally.
- Final delivery includes MP4, narration script, source/credit list, technical QA report, and 3–5 Shorts cut from the approved master.
