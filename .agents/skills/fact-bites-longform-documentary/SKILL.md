# FACT BITES Long-Form Documentary Skill

## Mission
Create original premium-feeling nature and science documentaries. Use cinematic natural-history storytelling as a quality reference, never copy another broadcaster's footage, script, music, presenter, branding, or distinctive sequences.

## Before production
1. Read `docs/FACT_BITES_DOCUMENTARY_STANDARD.md`.
2. Read the relevant episode brief under `docs/episodes/`.
3. Research claims using primary papers and authoritative scientific institutions.
4. Build a claim ledger: claim, exact source, evidence, uncertainty, narration wording.
5. Build a separate asset ledger: asset URL, creator/agency, license/reuse basis, credit, native resolution, scene assignment.
6. Reject any asset whose reuse rights are unclear. Publicly accessible is not equivalent to public domain.

## Story and script
- Target 8–12 minutes for early films, but never pad to reach a duration.
- Open with a striking image/question within 20 seconds.
- Use chapters with a clear progression and distinct visual purpose.
- Write for spoken English, with natural pauses and pronunciation notes.
- Explain scientific uncertainty and distinguish measurements, estimates, and hypotheses.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives and invented dialogue.

## Picture
- 16:9 landscape.
- Prefer native 4K; otherwise export at the best honest native source resolution.
- Do not upscale and market it as native 4K.
- Do not loop or repeat a small set of clips to fake a long documentary.
- Inspect frames from every scene for focus, relevance, framing, artifacts, and black frames.
- AI-generated wildlife visuals must not be presented as real footage.

## Voice and sound
- Use a warm, low-register, original English narrator voice.
- Never clone or imitate a named presenter.
- Use only original or rights-cleared music and field recordings.
- Keep narration intelligible; listen to beginning, middle, and end before approval.

## Deliverables
- Final MP4.
- Narration script.
- Research source ledger.
- Footage and audio credits.
- Technical QA report.
- 3–5 Shorts derived from the approved long-form master.

## Cost and truthfulness
- Use free tools and zero-cost assets only unless the user explicitly approves otherwise.
- Never silently fall back to paid services.
- Never say 4K, visually verified, audio checked, or ready unless that specific property has been tested.
- Preserve the existing short-form workflow; long-form production is a separate path.
