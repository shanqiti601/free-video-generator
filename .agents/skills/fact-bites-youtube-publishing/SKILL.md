# Fact Bites YouTube Publishing

Prepare metadata only after the script and video pass fact-check and QA.

## Deliverables
- One accurate title that creates curiosity without misleading viewers.
- A concise description with the central fact and source/footage credits.
- A short list of relevant search phrases.
- A small set of relevant hashtags; avoid hashtag stuffing.
- A pinned-comment question that invites meaningful discussion.
- A thumbnail/cover phrase of 2–5 words with strong mobile legibility.

## Rules
- Never promise a result the video does not deliver.
- Do not use unrelated trending keywords, misleading celebrity names, or fabricated claims.
- Preserve source credits and required license attribution.
- Do not publish automatically unless the user explicitly authorized publishing.
