# Fact Bites Documentary Script

Create an English-language vertical documentary short for Fact Bites.

## Editorial targets
- Aim for 45–60 seconds unless the task specifies otherwise.
- Open with a compelling but defensible curiosity hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Use a clear progression: hook, context, verified discovery, surprising implication, memorable close.
- Keep sentences natural for a deep, calm documentary narrator.
- Prefer concrete images, precise numbers, and plain language over hype.
- Explain uncertainty in a way that remains engaging.
- Avoid filler, repeated claims, fake suspense, and unsupported superlatives.
- End with a short, natural Fact Bites follow/subscribe line only when it fits.

## Deliverables
- Narration as one clean paragraph.
- Scene-by-scene visual plan using real, relevant footage where possible.
- On-screen caption chunks of 2–5 words, timed to narration.
- Sound design notes (subtle and original/licensed).
- Fact-check checklist with every claim that needs a source.

## Hard rules
- Do not write a script as fact until its source is verified.
- Never fabricate footage, archival material, expert quotes, or citations.
- Do not imply footage depicts an event unless it actually does.
- Respect public-domain, Creative Commons, and stock-footage license terms; record attribution requirements.
- Make the first 3 seconds understandable with sound off.
