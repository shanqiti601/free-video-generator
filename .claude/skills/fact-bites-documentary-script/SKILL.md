# Fact Bites Documentary Script

Create an English-language vertical documentary short for Fact Bites.

## Editorial targets
- Aim for 45–60 seconds unless specified otherwise.
- Open with a compelling, defensible spoken hook immediately; the first 3–5 seconds should use the strongest relevant footage and quick, clean cuts.
- Use a clear progression: hook, context, verified discovery, surprising implication, memorable close.
- Keep narration natural for a deep, calm documentary narrator.
- Prefer concrete images, precise numbers, and plain language over hype.
- Explain uncertainty without draining the story of momentum.
- Avoid filler, repeated claims, fake suspense, and unsupported superlatives.
- End with a memorable factual thought; no generic subscribe line unless explicitly requested.

## Visual identity
- On-screen text is limited to FACT BITES branding and concise FACT BITE cards with verified information.
- Do not burn in full narration subtitles.
- No generic opening title, slogan, CTA, or end card unless explicitly requested.
- Keep text within mobile-safe margins; use bright, natural exposure and restrained original sound design.

## Deliverables
- Narration as one clean paragraph.
- Scene-by-scene visual plan using real, relevant footage where possible.
- FACT BITE cards with exact timings and verified numbers.
- Sound design notes (subtle and original/licensed).
- Fact-check checklist with every claim that needs a source.

## Hard rules
- Do not write a script as fact until its source is verified.
- Never fabricate footage, archival material, expert quotes, or citations.
- Do not imply footage depicts an event unless it actually does.
- Respect public-domain, Creative Commons, and stock-footage license terms; record attribution requirements.
- Make the first 3 seconds understandable with sound off through the footage and a concise fact card, not a long title.
